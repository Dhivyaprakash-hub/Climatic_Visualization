02 / A NOTE ON METHOD

Good data needs
good context.

The CSV included in this repository contains 20 countries, one annual value and twelve monthly values for each. The accompanying coursework report identifies a 1991–2020 reference period and points to the World Bank Climate Change Knowledge Portal.

The workbook/export process and the exact indicator series are not preserved here. This explorer uses the supplied figures as-is and makes no claim that they are current, complete, or independently verified. For research, policy, or climate decisions, retrieve a documented series directly from the original data provider.
